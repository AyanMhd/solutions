Given two hourglasses of 4 minutes and 7 minutes, the task is to measure 9 minutes. 


Sol : Initially let both get empty
When 4 capacity one gets empty,
Reverse 4th one, now there are 3 & 4 capacity one (4 mins measured)
now when 3 capacity ones get empty, 7 mins measured
Reverse the 7 capacity glass
Now when 4 capacity one gets empty, 8 mins measured
Reverse both, 7 gets empty and 9 measured
