Given two candles that can be lit from either end. Each candle takes exactly one hour to burn completely  
when lit from a single end, even though it burns unevenly along its length. You also have a box of matches. 
Using only these candles and the matches, measure 45 minutes and 15 minutes.
 

Sol : Burn candle 1 at both ends and candle 2 at only one end 
When candle 1 is burnt completely, it is 30 mins 
Now light candle 2 at other end, when it burns completely, it is 15 mins.
