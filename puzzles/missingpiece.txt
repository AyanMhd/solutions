Three friends ordered a rectangular cake. One of them was in a hurry, so he cut a rectangular  
piece from the cake. The piece could be of any arbitrary size(less than the size of the cake) and rotation.

Now the remaining two friends want to divide the remaining cake into two equal portions with a single straight cut.

Note:

The single straight cut could pass through the cut-out portion.
They both need an equal amount of cream and toppings. Therefore,  
the cut must be made vertically from the top down. You cannot simply slice the cake  
in half horizontally through its middle (as you would a burger bun).

Sol : Due to symmetry, when a rectangle is cut through the centre, it always gets divided into half

So cut through a straight line from C(cut piece) & C(left_over)
