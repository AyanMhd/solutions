We have two water jugs, one measures 4 Gallons (4G) while the other measures 9 Gallons (9G). 
But there is no measuring label mentioned on either of these two jugs, i.e.,  
we cannot know the exact amount filled in the jug. 

Sol :  
For 4G and 9G, fill both completely 

For 5G : Empty 4G and fill 4G from 9G 

For 1G : Again empty 4G and fill 4G from 9G 

For 6G : Measured 1G, Full 9G and fill 4G  

For 2G : Empty 4G and fill through 6G filled 9G

For 7G : 2G 4G fill and full fill 9G, Now full fill 4G through 9G 

For 3G : Fill 4G when 9 is with 7 

For 6G : Fill 4g with 3g and 9 with 7 and done.
