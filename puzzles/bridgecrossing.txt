There are 4 people (A, B, C, and D) who want to cross a bridge at night.

A, B, C, and D take 1, 2, 5, and 8 minutes, respectively, to cross the bridge.
There is only one torch with them, and the bridge cannot be crossed without the torch.
There cannot be more than two people on the bridge at any time, and when two people cross the bridge together, 
they must move at the slower person's pace. 

Can they all cross the bridge in 15 minutes?

Solution : First cross 1,2 - 2 mins 

Then 1 goes back - 1 min 

Then 5,8 = 8 mins 

Then 2 goes back = 2 mins 

Then (1, 2) = 2 mins

Total time = 15 mins
