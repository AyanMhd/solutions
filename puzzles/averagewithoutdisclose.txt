Three Employees want to know the average of their salaries. They are not allowed to share their individual salaries.


Sol  :

  Let the salaries be a, b, c 

  a tells a + x to b, b tells b + y to c, c tells c + z to a 
  Now again, a tells c + z - x to b, b tells a + x - y to c and c b + y - z


  Sum these.
