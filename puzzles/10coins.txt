You are blindfolded, and 10 coins are placed on a table in front of you. You can touch and move the coins,  
but you cannot determine whether a coin is heads or tails by feeling it. You are told that exactly 5 coins  
are heads up and 5 coins are tails up, but you do not know which ones.

Your task is to divide the coins into two groups such that both groups have the same number of heads. 
You can flip the coins any number of times.


Sol : Make 2 sets (5 and 5 - x)

Flip all in either set and youre done
