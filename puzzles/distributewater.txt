Suppose there are three containers: container I (3 L), container II (5 L), and container III (8 L).  
Container III is filled with 8 L of water while the other two are empty.

With the help of these 3 containers, how do you distribute the water in equal volumes, i.e.,  
4 L in container II and 4 L in container III? Consider that there is no loss of water when  
transferring it from one container to another.

Sol :
        3, 2, 3
